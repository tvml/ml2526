### Bibliografia fondamentale

#### Bayesian Foundations
* T. Bayes, An Essay towards Solving a Problem in the Doctrine of Chances (1763)

* D.J.C. MacKay, Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms (Book, 2003)

#### Loss Functions, Risk Minimization
*  V. Vapnik, Statistical Learning Theory (Book, 1998)

#### Evaluation of ML Systems
* R. Kohavi, A Study of Cross-Validation and Bootstrap for Accuracy Estimation and Model Selection (IJCAI, 1995)

#### Generative/Discriminative Models, Logistic Regression
* R.A. Fisher, The Use of Multiple Measurements in Taxonomic Problems (Annals of Eugenics, 1936)

* D.R. Cox, The Regression Analysis of Binary Sequences (J. Royal Stat. Soc., 1958)

#### Regression (linear, nonlinear), Regularization
* A.N. Tikhonov, Solution of incorrectly formulated problems and the regularization method (Soviet Math. Dokl., 1963)

* R. Tibshirani, Regression Shrinkage and Selection via the Lasso (J. Royal Stat. Soc., 1996)

#### Linear Classifiers: Fisher LDA, Perceptron, Naive Bayes
* R.A. Fisher, The Use of Multiple Measurements in Taxonomic Problems (1936)

* F. Rosenblatt, The Perceptron: A Probabilistic Model for Information Storage and Organization in the Brain (Psychological Review, 1958)

#### SVMs, Kernel Methods
* Cortes & V. Vapnik, Support-Vector Networks (Machine Learning, 1995)

#### Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)
* D.E. Rumelhart, G.E. Hinton, R.J. Williams, Learning Representations by Back-Propagating Errors (Nature, 1986)

#### Decision Trees
* L. Breiman, J. Friedman, R. Olshen, C. Stone, Classification and Regression Trees (CART) (Book, 1984)

* J.R. Quinlan, Induction of Decision Trees (Machine Learning, 1986)

#### Ensemble Models: Bagging, Boosting
* L. Breiman, Bagging Predictors (Machine Learning, 1996)

* Y. Freund & R. Schapire, A Decision-Theoretic Generalization of On-Line Learning and an Application to Boosting (J. Comp. Syst. Sci., 1997)

* L. Breiman, Random Forests (Machine Learning, 2001)

#### Non parametric methods for regression and classification
* C. K. Williams, C. E. Rasmussen. Gaussian processes for regression. (Advances in neural information processing systems. 1996)

* C: E. Rasmussen. Gaussian processes for machine learning. The MIT Press, 2006.

* E. A. Nadaraya.  On Estimating Regression. (Theory of Probability and Its Applications. 1964)

* E.Parzen. On Estimation of a Probability Density Function and Mode. (Ann. Math. Statist. 1962)

*  T. M. Cover, P.E. Hart. Nearest neighbor pattern classification. (IEEE Transactions on Information Theory. 1967)

#### Clustering: k-means, Mixture Models
* S. Lloyd, Least Squares Quantization in PCM (IEEE Trans. Info Theory, 1982) 

* G.J. McLachlan & D. Peel, Finite Mixture Models (Book, 2000)

* G. Dempster, N. Laird, D. Rubin, Maximum Likelihood from Incomplete Data via the EM Algorithm (J. Royal Stat. Soc., 1977)

#### Dimensionality Reduction: PCA, Probabilistic PCA, Factor Analysis
* K. Pearson, On Lines and Planes of Closest Fit to Systems of Points in Space (Philosophical Magazine, 1901)

* I.T. Jolliffe, Principal Component Analysis (Book, 1986)

#### Introduction to Deep Learning: CNNs, RNNs, Attention/Transformers
* D.E. Rumelhart, G.E. Hinton, J.L. McClelland, Learning Internal Representations by Error Propagation (Parallel Distributed Processing, 1986) 

* Y. LeCun, B. Boser, J.S. Denker et al., Backpropagation Applied to Handwritten Zip Code Recognition (Neural Computation, 1989)

* S. Hochreiter & J. Schmidhuber, Long Short-Term Memory (Neural Computation, 1997)

* A. Vaswani et al., Attention Is All You Need (NeurIPS, 2017)
